# Choachi Wild — Project Context

## What is Choachi Wild?
A premium bio/ecotourism experience based in Choachí, Colombia (Eastern Andes, ~1h from Bogotá). The business guides international tourists through the páramo, high Andean cloud forest, Muisca ancestral heritage, and thermal springs — targeting European and North American markets. Website: choachiwild.com

## Business Model
- **Format:** Private, all-inclusive guided tours (small groups, no crowds)
- **Duration:** 2 Days / 1 Night
- **Price:** ~200 EUR / ~880,000 COP per person
- **Target market:** European (especially UK/Welsh) and North American travelers
- **Key differentiator:** Access to private land (RocaBlanca, Naranjalia) — no tour buses, no queues
- **Tone:** Premium, nature-first, culturally grounded — not mass tourism

## Key Locations & Stops
| Stop | Description |
|------|-------------|
| El Verjón Páramo | 3,400 masl — frailejones, rare ecosystem, water source for Bogotá |
| Mirador Peñas de la Bruja | Panoramic viewpoint of Eastern Cordillera |
| Mountain Breakfast | Traditional Cundiboyacense food (changua, arepa de choclo, chocolate santafereño) |
| RocaBlanca Expedition | 3h hike on private land — El Guardián Indígena (Muisca rock), giant anthuriums, siete cueros trees, Cascada del Chuscal |
| Termales Santa Mónica | Natural geothermal hot springs, afternoon recovery |
| Naranjalia | Private overnight refuge — bonfire dinner, local ingredients, mountain silence |
| Laguna de Ubaque | Sacred Muisca lake ~2,000 masl — connected to El Dorado legend |
| Fómeque | Colonial whitewashed village, 16th century |
| Río Blanco | Suspension bridge, optional swim, snowmelt river |
| El Turista Restaurant | Closing traditional lunch before return to Bogotá |

## Brand Voice & Values
- **Not** a checklist of attractions — "a coherent journey through one ecosystem, one culture"
- Authentic, not touristic ("not Bogotá, not Cartagena — the real thing")
- Respect for nature: stay on path, minimize impact, NTS-TS 002 sustainability compliance
- Muisca ancestral heritage is central (Guardián Indígena, Laguna de Ubaque, sacred sites)
- Gastronomy as cultural access point
- Contrast as a key experience hook: páramo mist in the morning → 38°C thermal water by afternoon

## Legal Notes (Colombia)
- Requires RNT (Registro Nacional de Turismo) for legal operation
- Guide needs Tarjeta Profesional de Guía de Turismo (Ley 300/1996)
- WFA/WFR wilderness first aid certification required
- Mandatory medical assistance insurance per passenger
